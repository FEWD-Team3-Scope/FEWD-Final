FAQ's

Registration proves ownership and ensures your car meets safety and legal requirements.

You usually need: - Driver’s license - Proof of insurance - Vehicle title - Smog or inspection certificate

Expired registration can lead to fines, late fees, and potential impoundment if the vehicle is driven on public roads.

You typically need the vehicle title, proof of insurance, a valid ID, and a completed registration application form.

It typically takes a few weeks, depending on processing times.
